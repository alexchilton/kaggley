## The pattern behind today's choice

From v10 to v14 the stacker held-out AUC rose 0.95019 -> 0.95091 (+0.00072), the board 0.95095 -> 0.95101 (+0.00006).
Every member's test prediction is the mean of 5 fold models fitted on 80% of the rows.
Four public notebooks measured a board gain from a full-data refit with the OOF unchanged (table below, idea 1).
HYPOTHESIS (unverified): our test side, not our OOF side, is what holds the board back.
v15 tests that: what it changes is only that three new members predict test from one fit on all 700k rows with encodings from all rows.
For it to win, the refit test predictions must rank test rows better than a 5-fold mean would; only the LB can show it.
